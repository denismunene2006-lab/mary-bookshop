FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you deliver outside Kilimani?

Yes. We deliver across Nairobi. Kilimani orders are free over KES 2,000; other areas are charged a small fee based on distance.

Can I order for a whole school or class?

Absolutely. Send us your supply list on WhatsApp and we'll prepare a custom quote with bundle discounts for bulk orders.

Do you stock the current Kenyan set books?

Yes — we keep up with the latest approved set books for both primary and secondary levels. Call ahead to confirm stock.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept M-Pesa, cash on delivery, and bank transfers for bulk orders. WhatsApp us and we'll share the details.
